Upload music to the Pi. Folders and songs appear on the MacroPad automatically.

The album folder and any subfolders are kept. On a phone, create an album folder above, open it, then select multiple songs with “Upload files”.

MacroPad keys

  • 1 Play (pulses)
  • 2 Pause
  • 3 Stop
  • 4 Change screen
  • 5 Back
  • 6 Play folder
  • 7 Spare
  • 8 Select
  • 9 Volume up
  • 10 Previous
  • 11 Next
  • 12 Volume down
